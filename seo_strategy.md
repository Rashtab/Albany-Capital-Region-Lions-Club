# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Public content pages, including blog posts and project pages
- Public discovery assets such as `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `llms.txt`

## Out of scope
- Authenticated and administrative routes under `/admin/**`
- API endpoints except where they affect crawlability or public content discovery

## Target audience
- Albany Capital Region community members
- Prospective club members
- Donors, sponsors, and event attendees
